PP2-BAL-10 feasibility is the incumbent (3,066,443 rational normalizations; 65 transports, all dual-assisted; 30 cold fallbacks). Its 8-identity cap does not bound serialized bytes. A component pool may retain rational strategy profiles and nonnegative dual multipliers after their source identity is evicted. The exact identity cache remains separate. No production changes.

## Mathematical contract

For target game g, ordered policy roster r and ending target t, a verified profile p=(x,y) with both unilateral regrets <= 1/5 yields lower value L(p,g)=TV(endingLaw(p,g),t). For nonempty proper ending subset S, define U_S(lambda,g)=min(1,max_cells(1[e in S]-lambda dot gaps_g(cell)))-t(S), lambda>=0. These are bounds used by amortized_verification.mjs. Include implicit zero dual per subset. Combining reusable components gives lower=max over target-certified profiles L and upper=max(0,max_S min over applicable duals U_S). Exact closure requires equality plus the existing target verifier; otherwise fall back independently. Store vectors only, with roster and subset tags. No stored interval or old feasibility flag is authoritative on a new game. Ending subset coverage and policy ordering must be verified. All-zero duals need not occupy component storage.

For games with identical ordered policies and entrywise payoff perturbation <= delta, either player's regret at a fixed profile changes by at most 2 delta. Thus a witness with source max regret <= 1/5 - 2 delta has a sufficient target feasibility margin; this is a proposal heuristic, never a replacement for verification. Changed ending labels and target distribution must still be evaluated. Complementarity matters: a witness and a missing dual component can each have zero closure value alone and positive value together. Do not assume closure reward is submodular or that a greedy approximation guarantee applies.

## Prototype scope

proof_component_memory.mjs is a standalone component store. It canonicalizes rational vectors, deduplicates within the exact roster, omits all-zero duals, validates inputs before admission, enforces a cap on UTF-8 JSON of persistent state (entries plus clock), and makes bounded joint packets with dual subset diversity. LRU and observed use frequency are implemented. The caller must provide verified source pools and call recordUse only after successful independent target verification. It is not a persistent disk service; the caller owns state lifetime, and runtime heap, scratch allocations, config, audit counters and identity cache are separate. The smoke is structural and does not execute a game or claim savings. The module does not replace the PP2-BAL-10 controller.

## Proposed prospective protocol

1. Train/design only using spent PP2-BAL-01..10 streams. Record roster, component payload bytes, identity-cache bytes, and peak total persistent serialized bytes. Freeze budgets and roster before fresh execution. Proposed byte grid: 2, 8, 32 KiB total, partition predeclared (e.g. 50/50 identity/component; compare whole-certificate controls using same total cap). An identity cap of eight remains a ceiling, not a guarantee. Charge all stored keys, metadata, and persistent estimator state. No unbounded source matrix, historical coverage table, or external controller oracle. Log runtime RSS separately.
2. Compare equal-total-byte identity-only LRU, whole-certificate feasibility retrieval, component LRU, seeded random eviction, component frequency and utility-aware eviction. Keep early pure and cold fallback identical. At most two packets per miss, with at most six profile checks and twelve nonzero dual candidates per packet; apply the same component-check limits to whole-source controls. Also cap ranking scans and meter every arithmetic/selection/encoding operation, including failures. Report candidate truncations. Include an equal-time comparison once wall time is measured.
3. For component coverage use train-only archetype summaries or online bounded counters. Estimate expected marginal saved work per byte conditional on other retained components, allowing witness/dual complementarity. Cold costs observed only on actual fallbacks; successful retrieval has no online counterfactual cold cost. Estimate from train-only or online fallback data, with audit-only counterfactual labels held outside the controller. Reward failed attempts negatively; credit successful exact proof components with a documented allocation rule. Pair every learned controller with frequency, random and recency controls under identical budgets. No arbitrary reward for maintaining an unused atom.
4. Reuse the existing PP2-BAL-10 six roots only as spent development replays. For prospective evaluation generate and freeze at least two structurally new admissible families with different transition topology/policy rosters, multiple roots and shared candidate streams. Add long-horizon recurrence and roster-change stress; prevent cross-roster transport unless a separately verified permutation transform exists. Report per-root paired savings, cold fallback and identity rates, bytes, profile and dual checks, total work, bit lengths and wall time. Independently recompute cold after each trajectory and reverify every output; require interval containment and preservation of witness availability. Keep audit objects unreachable from controllers. Closed-loop authoring is a separate experiment.
5. Promotion requires zero invalid or weakened certificates, no capacity or retrieval violations, and paired savings over the strongest equal-byte fixed baseline with all online work charged. Predeclare the primary budget and uncertainty analysis by independent root. Learned utility eviction is an experimental arm; its current advantage is unknown.
