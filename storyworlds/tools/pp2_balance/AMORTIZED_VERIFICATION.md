# PP2-BAL-09: amortized strategic verification
Frozen development replay protocol. Use the six PP2-BAL-08 fresh-root receipts (now spent), parent-priority and random-global trajectories, 64 queries each, 768 queries total. Replay the exact same matrices and targets in every arm; earlier CI regenerates those matrices from the pinned native reader. Native reader enumeration, author loss, perception sensitivity and extraction are shared and unchanged. No live search acceleration or end-to-end wall-time claim.

Arms: cold full independent PP2-BAL-05 screen and verification on every query; identity cache keyed by both rational matrices, ending cells, exact policy IDs, canonical target and epsilon; identity plus early pure witness screen (try only cells attaining the trivial support upper bound, stop on certified witness); parent transport plus same early-pure/full fallback; bank transport plus same fallback. Parent means actual recorded proposal source, not the last visited grid. Bank uses at most eight prior unique game proofs, parent first, then most recently accessed identities. Per-trajectory cache and bank reset, no cross-root/cross-arm sharing. Exact-game hits reuse an already verified in-memory result. All misses require independent proof verification. No use of the cold answer in the optimized arms.

For new games, recheck prior rational strategy profiles on the edited matrices. If a surviving witness achieves the trivial support upper bound, stop. Otherwise for each nonempty proper ending subset S, reuse nonnegative dual multipliers from previous proof objects (also consider zero), compute the exact upper envelope max_cell(indicator_S-lambda dot gaps), and choose the tightest upper per subset. The maximum over subsets after subtracting target mass bounds worst TV. Accept transported result only if a feasible witness attains that upper; otherwise run early pure then full independent screen. Stored transport proofs contain target witness and selected subset multiplier vectors; independent verifier recomputes the witness and all upper envelopes. Policy roster mismatch blocks transport. Exactness here is for the restricted rational bimatrix game and author utilities under the reader semantics. Every output interval compared to a separately executed cold result after the arm; no weaker intervals, no conflicting exact values, no loss of known feasible witness. Old screens replay exactly. Unit exercises mixed dominant-action solution with nonzero dual and five tampered proofs. No proof of global search equivalence when a tighter interval changes ranking.

Meter only strategic solver/verifier work. Optional counters in strategic.mjs, strategic_robustness.mjs and robust_authoring.mjs count rational normalization calls (including parsing), rational comparison calls, regret evaluations (including internal solver calls), public certifies, envelope and solver calls, bilinear profile/corner evaluations. Transport also counts its own normalized fractions, comparisons, dual cells and witness checks. These are implementation-level operation counts, not bit-operation or wall-time estimates; unmetered JSON/cache, sorting, BigInt primitive work, and native reader costs excluded. Independent post-run oracle checks outside the arm meters. Report actual counts, exact transport hits, identity hits, early-pure hits, cold fallback and bounded bank occupancy. No native TRM/BPD training. Replay node amortized_replay.mjs; --write regenerates receipts. No production changes.
