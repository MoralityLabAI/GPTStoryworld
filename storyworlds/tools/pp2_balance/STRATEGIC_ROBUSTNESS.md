# PP2-BAL-04: equilibrium robustness certificates

For every nonempty proper subset S of the ending alphabet, let F_S(x,y)=Pr(ending in S). Over the independent mixed-policy profiles with both unilateral regrets <= 1/5, certify
L_S <= sup F_S <= U_S.
Then the minimum ending probability has bounds 1-U_complement <= inf F_single <= 1-L_complement, and the maximum has bounds L_single <= sup F_single <= U_single. Worst-case target TV is max_S(sup F_S - q(S)), so its certified interval is [max_S(L_S-q(S)), max_S(U_S-q(S))]. All quantities are rational, conditional on the admitted two-player game, exact terminal utility sidecars and deterministic floating-point native reader replay. The witness for one subset may differ from another.

## Global relaxation and proof
For every joint pure cell (i,j), define row deviations g_k(i,j)=R[k,j]-R[i,j]-1/5 and column deviations h_l(i,j)=C[i,l]-C[i,j]-1/5. The expectation of each gap under independent mixed root policies is <=0 exactly when the corresponding deviation is within threshold. Dropping independence yields a linear outer relaxation. For any vector lambda>=0, max_cell (indicator_S - lambda dot gaps) is an upper bound for all feasible independent profiles. Coordinate descent minimizes this convex piecewise-linear dual bound along each multiplier, using exact rational intersections. It need not find the optimal LP dual. The verifier reconstructs the matrix of gaps and checks every inequality and nonnegative multiplier. This does not equate correlated and independent equilibria.

## Cover refinement
Partition the product of simplices by longest-edge bisection of one factor simplex at a time. Replace one endpoint by the exact midpoint in each of the two child simplices; their union is the parent. Every gap and subset mass is bilinear, hence its extrema on a product cell occur at vertex pairs. Exclude a cell only if one gap is positive at every vertex pair. All remaining cells contribute their corner maxima to the upper bound, capped by the global dual bound. A compact preorder tree (s=split, x=excluded, l=live) certifies coverage; the verifier independently reconstructs the partition and rechecks each exclusion and all surviving upper bounds. Dyadic integer corner arithmetic is exact. Search may stop early or at its cap; unresolved gaps remain explicit. Search priority uses floating-point approximations only to choose a cell, never to certify a bound.

## Lower witnesses
Recheck all solver seeds with the exact regret verifier. Move along one-player segments toward pure and uniform policies; feasibility on a segment is an intersection of affine inequalities, so the maximum feasible step is rational. Keep feasible corner witnesses from refinement, too. Each stored witness is independently checked and its ending-subset mass recomputed. No claim of global optimality unless lower=upper.

## Pilot and limitations
Uses PP2-BAL-03's committed native-reader matrix receipts, comparing target-aligned profiles on original versus selected author-loss-preserving candidates. Same six coefficient roots, one structural family, author-chosen synthetic utilities and restricted policy rosters. The proof unit covers exact dominance (bad ending <=1/5), matching pennies refinement and rejected tampered dual/witness/cover. Max 256 splits per distinct game, depth 14, four dual and witness sweeps. Finite 2-6 ending alphabet and at most 64 joint policy cells. This is a verifier-grounded practical extension of Cogentic's 1/5 threshold, not a claim that its Nash existence theorem guarantees a target distribution, robust narrative balance, or higher-order/multiplayer equilibrium. Production worlds are unchanged. Candidate selection is not yet driven by worst-case TV. Receipts are deterministic; replay via `node storyworlds/tools/pp2_balance/strategic_robustness_pilot.mjs`. Native reader matrices are replayed by the preceding PP2-BAL-03 CI step. At commit creation the new proof and pilot ran in a JS isolate; hosted CI pending.
