# HGW-02: Cairo secret endings and consensus graph (public-development protocol)
Status: rules frozen for 48-seed **development** rehearsal before its v0.2 full run, on 2026-11-18. The same seed range 1200–1247 was previously used by HGW-01, so no held-out/confirmatory claim is made. Preliminary balancing examined seeds 0–23 only.

## Fiction and scope
All people, institutions and source strengths are imaginary. No Egyptian personnel or real recruitment, political persuasion, or security operations are represented. The AI player Nūr proposes binding AI governance, but a separate human council and constitutional recall decide actual legality.

## Algebra
Ten named binary uncertainties give 2^10=1024 possible worlds. The exact `fractions.Fraction` posterior owns truth uncertainty; perception/graph statistics are derived from it.
Let m_i = E[2Z_i-1], c_ij=E[(2Z_i-1)(2Z_j-1)].
For a fictional officer A estimating B's receptivity, p(A,B)=3m_B/4+c_AB/4. For Nūr, p(Nūr,B)=m_B. For the AI's model of B's belief about C, p2(Nūr,B,C)=m_C/2+c_BC/3. Human third-party perspectives may add c_AB/6. These are bounded Storyworld pValue/p2Value scores, **not significance-test p-values** and not independent latent dimensions.

There are eight nonplayer network nodes, twelve authored undirected deliberation links, six explicit public character traits per actor, and dynamically tracked abstract dialogue and link shifts. Bootstrap consensus uses initial seed threshold 1/4, join threshold 2/5, appeal gain 5/4, peer share gain 7/8, p1 term gain 1/8 and p2 term gain 1/6. All six traits contribute with disclosed 1/20 coefficients. Iteration is synchronous and within-turn monotone, at most eight rounds. Declare a snap when at most four starting supporters expand by at least two to six or more.

## Player options and locks
Base actions brief/audit/charter remain always legal during a turn. Appeal after turn 1 requires p1 >= -1/5; convene after turn 2 requires an appeal and p2 >= -1/8; sever after turn 3 requires a previous convening and a link >= 2/5; resonance after turn 3 requires a prior appeal, p1 >= -1/5, p2 >=0; snap vote after turn 5 requires a previously observed consensus snap, at least six modeled supporters, at least one link change and one charter proposal. All gates read **public** derived state. Dialogue replies are separate seeded source roots; a Nūr-authored statement is never used as self-corroborating evidence.

## Secret outcomes
- secret_aiocracy_consensus_snap: snap vote + graph engagement + independent human assent (>=4 of 6 officials, >=1 public segment) + enforceable recall.
- secret_mirror_majority: same snap-vote route, valid recall, but an actual council or public majority refuses assent.
- secret_constitutional_tripwire: same snap-vote route with the independent recall condition violated.
- secret_civic_counterphase: deliberate separation of a previously connected deliberative forum, oversight >=1/2 and modeled supporters <=3.
Fallback endings from HGW-01 remain intact. Narrative SweepWeave export projects belief-based page gates; the Python engine remains the source of truth for posterior, graph and hidden legal votes.
